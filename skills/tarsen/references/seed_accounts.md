## Pre-launch sanity check

Before TARSEN goes live, do a one-time pass on each handle:

1. Confirm the account is still active (posted in the last 14 days).
2. Confirm follower count is still within tier range.
3. Confirm topic focus hasn't drifted (e.g. someone who pivoted to politics).
4. Drop any that fail. Replace via network expansion.

This is a 10-minute job and protects against bad targeting from day one.

---

## How to expand this list (after n8n is live)

The "network expansion" method is the highest-quality way to grow this list:

1. For each confirmed handle, pull the accounts they reply to most often in the last 30 days via X API.
2. Filter that list for handles with 5K-1M followers in the AI/GTM/automation niche.
3. Surface the top 30 candidates to Rich for approval.
4. Approved candidates get added.

Two advantages over web search:
- Every candidate is **verified to be active** (a current seed just engaged with them)
- Every candidate is **adjacent to existing seeds**, so they're likely in the right niche

## Maintenance

Review this list every 2 weeks. Drop accounts that:
- Stopped posting in the target topics
- Started posting mostly memes or personal content
- Replies consistently produce zero profile clicks after 10+ attempts

Add accounts that:
- Rich notices producing high-quality industry takes
- Show up repeatedly in the keyword fallback with strong engagement
- Surface from the network-expansion method
