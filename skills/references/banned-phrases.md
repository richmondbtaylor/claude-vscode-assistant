# Banned Phrases & Words — Do Not Use

This is the authoritative list of overused, AI-sounding, corporate, and salesy words/phrases that must NEVER appear in any Bishop AI content output.

## Classic AI / Tech Buzzwords
- Game changer / game-changing
- Paradigm shift
- Disruptive / disruption
- Revolutionize / revolutionary
- Cutting-edge / bleeding-edge
- Next-level
- At the forefront
- Transformative
- Innovative solution / innovative
- Pioneering
- State-of-the-art
- Best-in-class / world-class
- Future-proof
- Scalable / Robust (as vague praise)
- Seamless / frictionless
- "Harness the power of..."
- Unlock (unlock your potential, unlock growth, unlock value)
- Leverage (as a verb)
- Streamline
- Accelerate / Amplify (when abstract)
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "The future is now"
- Delve into
- "It goes without saying"

## Corporate Speak / Meeting Room Jargon
- Synergy
- Bandwidth (used for humans)
- Circle back / touch base
- Move the needle
- Low-hanging fruit
- Deep dive / double down
- Boil the ocean
- Alignment / get aligned
- "At the end of the day"
- Actionable insights
- Learnings (not a real word)
- Stakeholder buy-in
- Value proposition (as filler)
- Swim lanes
- Ideate / operationalize / productize
- Cadence (for meetings)

## Bro Marketing / Hustle Culture
- Crushing it
- 10x (as a verb)
- Level up (when abstract)
- No-brainer
- Secret sauce
- Pain points (overused)
- "This changes everything"
- "You're leaving money on the table"
- Dominate your niche / own your space
- Skyrocket / explode your growth
- Go viral (as an empty goal)
- Thought leader (self-applied)
- Ecosystem (when it's just a product)
- Community (when it's just a Slack group)
- "The [X] space" (the AI space, the wellness space)
- Done-for-you / plug and play / set it and forget it

## LinkedIn / Newsletter Epidemic Phrases
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "Humbled and honored"
- "Unpopular opinion:"
- "Let that sink in."
- "Here's what nobody tells you:"
- "Hot take:" / "Real talk:"
- "We need to talk about..."
- "Stop doing X. Start doing Y."
- "Obsessed with this"
- Adding value / showing up / doing the work (as hollow phrases)

## AI-Sounding Openers
- Certainly! / Absolutely! / Of course!
- "Great question!"
- "It's important to note that..."
- "I'd be happy to help with that!"
- "As an AI language model..."
- "In today's digital landscape..."
- "In conclusion" (on short answers)
- "Tailored to your unique needs"
- "This comprehensive guide"
- "In the realm of..."
- "Navigating [anything]"
- Holistic approach / multifaceted / robust framework
- Foster (foster growth, foster connection, foster innovation)

## Hall of Shame
- Democratize (when the thing costs money)
- Empower (as vague filler)
- Impact (as a verb)
- Authentic / authenticity (as marketing)
- Omnichannel / hyperlocal / disintermediate
- Full-stack (applied to humans)
- At scale (as vague buzzword)
- Bite-sized / curated (as vague descriptors)
- Utilize (use "use")
- Facilitate / enhance / optimize (when abstract)
- Unprecedented / groundbreaking
- Comprehensive / dynamic / impactful (as vague descriptors)

## The Rule
If a phrase could appear in a generic AI-generated marketing email, a LinkedIn post from a stranger, or a corporate press release — don't use it. Write like a smart, direct person explaining something to a colleague. Be specific. Use plain language. Say exactly what you mean.
