LINKEDIN CAPTION

I've been building automation workflows for clients for 8 months. Here's the counterintuitive thing I keep learning:

The simple workflows save the most time.

Last week I built a 4-node n8n workflow for a sales team. It does three things:
- Watches a shared inbox for new lead emails
- Extracts contact info and logs it to a Google Sheet
- Fires a Slack notification to the assigned sales rep

Build time: 25 minutes.

The team lead's feedback: their junior SDRs are saving 3 hours a day on manual data entry.

Three hours. Per person. Per day.

There's a temptation — especially in the current AI hype cycle — to think that valuable automation has to be sophisticated. That if it isn't a complex multi-agent pipeline, it doesn't count.

It doesn't work that way.

The best first automation isn't the most impressive one. It's the one that removes the most friction from your team's actual day.

So if you're thinking about where to start: don't look for the big, exciting use case. Look for the thing your team hates doing every week. That's your first workflow.

What's the most unexpectedly simple automation you've seen deliver big results? I'd love to hear it in the comments.
