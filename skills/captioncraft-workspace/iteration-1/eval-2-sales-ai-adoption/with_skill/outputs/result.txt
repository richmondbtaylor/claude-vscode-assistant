Your sales team isn't ignoring your AI tool because they're resistant to change.

They're ignoring it because you didn't give them a reason to care.

I've worked with 15–20 sales teams in the last year and a half who bought some AI tool — email writers, call summarizers, you name it — and six months later, nobody's using it.

When I dig into why, the answer is almost always the same: leadership bought the tool to make the team more "efficient." In rep-speak, that means "so we can fire some of you." And reps picked up on that energy immediately.

Rule #1 of AI adoption in sales: the reps have to win first. If your AI tool makes their quota more achievable and saves them time, they'll use it. If it feels like surveillance, they'll quietly ignore it.

Rule #2: stop dropping a login link in Slack and calling it a rollout. Real adoption means integrating the tool into their existing workflow as a replacement for something they already hate doing. Not as an add-on.

Win the rep first. Replace, don't add.
