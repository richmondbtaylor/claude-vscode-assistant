LINKEDIN CAPTION

I've seen it happen over and over: a sales leader buys an AI tool, rolls it out to the team, and 6 months later nobody's using it.

The technology isn't the problem. The adoption is.

After working with 15-20 sales teams, I've found two root causes almost every time:

1. Reps didn't see what was in it for them. When leadership talks about "efficiency," reps hear "headcount reduction." They pick up on that energy and quietly opt out.

2. The tool was never properly integrated. Dropping a login link in Slack is not a rollout. Real adoption means showing people exactly when and how to use it in their actual day — and replacing something they hate doing, not adding to their plate.

The fix? Win the rep first. Make the tool serve them. Make it easier to hit quota. Then integrate it where it replaces friction, not where it adds more.
