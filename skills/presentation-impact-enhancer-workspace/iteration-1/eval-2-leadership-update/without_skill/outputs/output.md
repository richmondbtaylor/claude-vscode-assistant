# Presentation Feedback: Q1 2026 AI Initiative Update

## Overall Assessment

This is a functional status update presentation that covers the essential bases — budget, milestones, risks, and next steps. However, it reads more like an internal project tracker than a leadership communication. The information is present but largely unframed: there is no narrative thread, no stated objective, and no clear ask. A leadership audience will want to know not just what happened, but what it means and what you need from them.

---

## Slide-by-Slide Feedback

### Slide 1: Q1 2026 AI Initiative Update

**What works:**
- The "On Track" status label is useful and immediately visible.
- Budget and team size are relevant anchors for a leadership audience.

**What needs improvement:**

- **No context or framing.** The slide does not tell the audience what this initiative is, why it matters, or what success looks like.
- **The budget figure is ambiguous.** "$240k of $300k used" with no timeline reference is unclear — is this on pace or overspending relative to schedule?
- **"On Track" is asserted, not supported.** Add a one-line rationale or make it clear the following slides will validate it.
- **Team composition lacks purpose.** "8 engineers, 2 PMs" is a raw fact without context around capacity or impact of the upcoming departure.

### Slide 2: Key Milestones

**What works:**
- Clear status labels and specific dates add credibility.

**What needs improvement:**
- Milestones are engineering activities with no stated business significance.
- "Phase 1" implies a phase 2 that is never addressed.
- The Mar 20 API gateway deadline is 7 days away — warrants a confidence note given active risks.
- Beta launch should list a readiness condition, not just a date.

### Slide 3: Risks & Blockers

**What works:**
- Three scoped risks with one quantified (12% overrun).

**What needs improvement:**
- No severity or likelihood ratings — all risks appear equal weight.
- No mitigation described for the cost overrun.
- Engineer departure timeline and downstream impact are unspecified.
- Third-party API rate limits lack a resolution path.
- No escalation or decision request to leadership.

### Slide 4: Next Steps

**What works:**
- Three time-bound actions listed. April 15 board date is a useful anchor.

**What needs improvement:**
- No owners assigned to any next step.
- Pilot customer onboarding is underspecified (confirmed? contracted?).
- No connection between next steps and the risks on the prior slide.
- Q2 board presentation is mentioned without telling leadership what input is needed from them.

---

## Structural Feedback

- **Missing: Opening narrative** — stakes, context, and why this update matters.
- **Missing: Clear ask** — this presentation does not drive a decision.
- **Missing: Closing summary slide** — leave the audience with a clear mental model and explicit next action.

---

## Priority Recommendations

1. Add a one-sentence initiative summary to Slide 1.
2. Address the budget overrun with a mitigation plan, not just a flag.
3. Assign owners to all next steps.
4. Add a closing slide with a clear ask.
5. Connect risks to milestones explicitly to show integrated thinking.
