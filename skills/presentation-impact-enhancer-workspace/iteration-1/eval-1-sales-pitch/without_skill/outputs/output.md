# Bishop AI — Sales Pitch Deck: Enhanced Presentation Feedback

## Overview

**Outcome:** Sales pitches to new clients
**Original Slides:** 5
**Assessment:** The deck has a solid logical flow but lacks emotional resonance, specificity, and proof points that convert skeptical buyers. Below is a slide-by-slide critique followed by a fully rewritten version.

---

## Slide-by-Slide Critique

### Slide 1: Title Slide
**Current:** "Bishop AI — Intelligent Automation for Modern Teams"

**Issues:**
- "Intelligent Automation" and "Modern Teams" are overused buzzwords that signal nothing unique.
- No subheadline to orient the audience or hint at the value proposition.
- Missing a hook — the title should make a prospect lean forward.

**Recommendation:** Lead with a specific outcome, not a category label. Add a punchy subheadline that speaks to the audience's pain.

---

### Slide 2: The Problem
**Issues:**
- The "40% of their day" stat is uncited — sophisticated buyers will question it.
- Bullet points are flat declarations with no narrative tension.
- "High cost of custom development" is buried as a fourth point but is often the most compelling pain.
- No personalization — this could describe any company in any industry.

**Recommendation:** Open with a relatable scenario. Cite the stat source. Lead with the costliest pain.

---

### Slide 3: Our Solution
**Issues:**
- "Automates your workflows" is vague — what kind of workflows?
- "100+ tools" needs a logo wall or named examples to be credible.
- "ROI in 30 days" is a bold claim with zero substantiation — it will trigger skepticism.
- No differentiation from competitors like Zapier, Make, or Workato.

**Recommendation:** Name 2–3 specific use cases. Show recognizable integration logos. Back the ROI claim with a brief case study.

---

### Slide 4: How It Works
**Issues:**
- The three steps are abstract — "Define your workflow" tells the buyer nothing about effort.
- "Monitor results in real time" is a feature, not a benefit.

**Recommendation:** Pair each step with a concrete time estimate. Add a screenshot placeholder. Reframe monitoring as "catch issues before they cost you."

---

### Slide 5: Pricing
**Issues:**
- No value anchoring — prices appear before the buyer fully believes in the ROI.
- The free trial offer is undersold — it's the strongest CTA and deserves prominence.
- No feature differentiation between tiers.

**Recommendation:** Highlight the most popular tier. Add 2–3 key features per tier. Make the free trial the closing CTA with urgency framing.

## Top 5 High-Impact Improvements

1. Replace buzzwords with outcomes — every headline should answer "so what?"
2. Cite your statistics — one sourced stat outperforms ten unsourced ones in sales contexts
3. Name your integrations — logo walls eliminate "will it work with our stack?" objections
4. Substantiate ROI claims — a single case study sentence converts skeptics better than assertions
5. Elevate the free trial CTA — it is your strongest conversion tool and deserves dominant visual placement
