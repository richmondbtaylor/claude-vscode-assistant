## SLIDE 3 — The Insight

**Headline:**
Energy matters more than time.

**Body:**
The same 2 hours can produce very different results depending on WHEN you use them. Morning you is not afternoon you. Plan around your energy, not just your availability.

**Visual Direction:** Split graphic: sharp morning brain vs foggy afternoon brain.

---

## SLIDE 4 — Block 1

**Headline:** Block 1: Deep Work (Morning)

**Body:** This is your most valuable real estate. Reserve it for your hardest, highest-leverage tasks. No meetings. No email. No exceptions.

---

## SLIDE 5 — Block 2

**Headline:** Block 2: Admin (Midday)

**Body:** Email. Slack. Quick calls. Scheduling. This is where reactive work lives -- not your morning. Your energy dips here anyway. Use it wisely.

---

## SLIDE 6 — Block 3

**Headline:** Block 3: Creative (Afternoon)

**Body:** Content. Brainstorming. Strategy. Exploration. Creative work doesn't need peak focus -- it needs a relaxed mind. Let it breathe here.

---

## SLIDE 7 — The Rule

**Headline:** The one rule that changes everything: No meetings before 11am.

**Body:** Your first block is sacred. The moment you let a meeting own your morning, someone else's urgency becomes your priority.

**Visual Direction:** Bold, centered text. Red barrier visual.

---

## SLIDE 8 — The Reframe

**Headline:** Saying no to a meeting IS a productivity strategy.

**Body:** Not every meeting needs you. Not every request deserves your best hours. Your calendar should reflect your values -- not other people's urgencies.

**Visual Direction:** "NO" in large confident text. Clean, assertive design.

**Body:** Look at your last week. Does it reflect what matters most to you? Or does it reflect what was most urgent to everyone around you?
