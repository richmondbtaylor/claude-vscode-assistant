### PLATFORM VARIATIONS

**Instagram**: Use the Curiosity hook (option 1) for maximum scroll-stop. Keep all text in the upper 2/3 of each slide. The myth-bust angle on slide 2 works well as a Reels cover crossover. The engagement mechanic on slide 4 drives comment replies mid-swipe.

**LinkedIn**: Use the Contrarian hook (option 2) -- it signals a confident professional POV which performs well in the feed. Slide 5 can include a parenthetical like "(I wasted years chasing discipline instead of this)" if desired. CTA on slide 8 can add: "Tag someone who needs to hear slide 6."

---

### ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY
- **Hook angle**: Contrarian + pattern interrupt -- "motivation isn't your problem" directly challenges the default self-improvement narrative, triggering cognitive dissonance and stopping the scroll.
- **Mid-carousel hooks**: Slide 4 plants a direct question ("Have you tried protecting your mornings?") at the midpoint to prime comment intent before the CTA lands.
- **CTA type**: Open-ended reflective question ("Does your calendar reflect your values?") paired with "I read every one" to signal responsiveness and lower the barrier to commenting.
- **Save-worthiness**: The 3-block framework is concrete and repeatable -- structured enough to screenshot and return to. Slide 7's reframe ("calendar = values") is quotable and share-worthy on its own.
