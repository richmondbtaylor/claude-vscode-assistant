### ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY
- **Hook angle**: Contrarian/direct — calls out the reader's behavior immediately, creates mild tension that pulls them in
- **Mid-carousel hooks**: Slide 3 uses a three-beat reveal to mirror the reader's own experience (ask, answer, quit) — high relatability trigger; Slide 6 reframes failure as feedback — surprise/perspective shift
- **CTA type**: Save-first CTA on a dark high-contrast final slide; secondary comment prompt gives an engagement safety net if they don't save
- **Save-worthiness**: Checklist-style content entrepreneurs will want to reference before using AI tools; each mistake is actionable and specific enough to be immediately useful without being overwhelming
