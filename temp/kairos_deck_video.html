KAIROS
Definition

A daemon agent.

Plain English: a program that runs in the background all the time, even when your laptop is closed and you're nowhere near a keyboard.

$ ps aux | grep kairos  ✓ running

What it watches for

Continuous codebase monitoring.

Bugs

Logic errors and broken paths.

Performance

Slow paths and bottlenecks.

Dependencies

Stale and risky packages.

Security

Vulnerabilities and CVEs.

It doesn't just flag

It opens the PR.

When Kairos finds something, it doesn't drop a ticket. It writes the fix, opens a branch, and pushes a pull request straight to your queue. While you sleep.
