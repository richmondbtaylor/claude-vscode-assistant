DREAM
Why it's called Dream

Sleep is when memory consolidates.

Neuroscience research has documented this for years. While you sleep, your brain prunes weak memories, merges related ones, and strengthens the patterns that matter.

Dream does the same thing for your AI, between sessions.

What it learns about you

Not because you told it. Because it watched.
