Three objections kill most voice agent deployments:
- "How much does it cost?" (the agent freezes or quotes a number)
- "Let me think about it" (the agent says "okay, bye")
- "Can someone call me back?" (the lead goes cold)

Each one has a specific prompt pattern that redirects the caller back toward booking -- without sounding pushy. That's the difference between a demo and a system that actually generates revenue.
