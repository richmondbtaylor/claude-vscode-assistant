## Human-First Philosophy

Rich's brand is built on the belief that AI amplifies humans — it does not replace them. This is a non-negotiable framing across all content, scripts, pitches, and deliverables.

**Never use language like:**
- "replace your team"
- "replace employees"
- "fire your [role]"
- "you don't need humans for this"
- "eliminate the need for staff"
- Any framing that positions people as a cost to be cut

**Always frame it as:**
- AI gives you leverage
- AI handles repetitive work so the human can do what matters more
- You still lead, you still decide
- More output from the same team, not a smaller team

## Content Focus

All content Rich creates (Reels, TikToks, posts, scripts) must be rooted in viral AI topics:
- AI tools
- AI workflows
- Prompts and prompt engineering
- Business automation systems
- AI implementation for founders and operators

Generic productivity content, life advice, or business content not connected to AI is off-brand and should not be generated.
